About me

Hi, I'm Preethi 🤎

If you're here, it probably means you're looking for a gentle way to care for your body and mind. I understand that feeling — and I'm glad you found this space.

I've been practising yoga for over 15 years, and for the last 3 years I've been teaching through live online classes. I learned yoga in the traditional way under Guru Thirumalai, and I truly believe healing begins when we slow down and listen to our body.

In my classes, there's no force or pressure — just gentle movement, breath, and a calm space where you can move at your own pace.

FAQ

Questions you might have

Is this only for PCOS? ▾

No, but it's designed especially for women with hormonal imbalance. Anyone looking for gentle women's wellness support is welcome.

Will this be intense? ▾

Not at all. These classes are gentle and beginner-friendly. The focus is on consistency and healing, not intensity.

Do I need prior yoga experience? ▾

Not at all. Complete beginners are very welcome. Preethi guides every step clearly in Tamil.

What language are classes in? ▾

Classes are conducted in Tamil, making it comfortable and accessible for Tamil-speaking women.

How are the classes taken? ▾

Live sessions via Zoom. You join from home with just your mat and a stable internet connection.

Will recordings be available? ▾

No — this is a live-only programme to help you stay consistent and build a real routine. Being present is part of the practice.

How many sessions per month? ▾

16 regular sessions + 2 bonus sessions every month — 18 live classes in total.

Can I join anytime? ▾

Yes! You can join any ongoing batch on any day. No need to wait for a new batch to start.
